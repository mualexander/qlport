\begin{remark}[How v2 was obtained, and the table of repairs]\label{rem:inference-table}
Grammar v1 was written from the documentation and the published examples alone, before
the corpus existed. Running it over the corpus (\S\ref{sec:evaluation}) left a
substantial share of real queries unparsed, and the question is what to conclude from a
failure. Rather than classify failures by hand, each is explained by the smallest set of
\emph{repairs} that makes it parse, where every repair is one concrete production or
terminal change. Grammar v2 is v1 with all of those changes applied. Each row of
Table~\ref{tab:grammar-gaps} is therefore verified twice: the construct fails under v1
and is explained by exactly that repair, and v2 parses the same construct with no repair
at all. The residual --- queries no combination of repairs explains --- is reported
rather than absorbed.
