\begin{remark}[One gap per query]\label{rem:one-gap}
Lowering abandons a query at its first untranslatable construct, and the
$\bot$ it returns carries that one \texttt{Gap}. So the catalogue of
\S\ref{sec:gaps} counts \emph{first} refusals: a query with two problems appears under
whichever is reached first, and the per-code counts are lower bounds on how many queries
each construct affects. What they are not lower bounds on is the total --- every refused
query is counted exactly once --- so $\kappa$ is unaffected, and the ordering of
\S\ref{sec:staircase} is computed from features rather than from gap codes for this
reason.
\end{remark}
