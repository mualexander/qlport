\begin{definition}[Denotation]\label{def:ir-sem}
Fix a database $D$ and a time $t$. Writing $\lambda(s)$ for a series' label set and
$s|_{(a,b]}$ for its samples in that interval:
\begin{align*}
\sem{\mathrm{Select}(m,\overline{\mu},\theta)}(D,t)
  &= \{\, s\in D \;:\; \mathrm{name}(s)=m,\ \lambda(s)\models\overline{\mu} \,\}\\
\sem{\mathrm{Window}(e,w)}(D,t) &= \{\, (\lambda(s),\ s|_{(t-w,\,t]}) \;:\; s\in\sem{e}(D,t) \,\}\\
\sem{\mathrm{Rollup}(\mathit{op},\Delta,e,b)}(D,t)
  &= \{\, (\lambda(s),\ \mathit{op}(s|_{[t,\,t+\Delta)})) \;:\; s\in\sem{e}(D,t) \,\}
\end{align*}
For space aggregation let $\pi_{\overline{k}}$ keep the labels in $\overline{k}$ under
$\mathit{mode}=\mathsf{by}$ and drop them under $\mathsf{without}$, and let $G_\lambda$
collect the values of $\sem{e}(D,t)$ whose label set agrees with $\lambda$ on
$\overline{k}$; then
\[
\sem{\mathrm{Agg}(\mathit{op},\overline{k},\mathit{mode},e)}(D,t)
  = \{\,(\pi_{\overline{k}}(\lambda),\ \mathit{op}(G_\lambda)\,/\,\Delta^{[b]})\,\}.
\]
Here $\Delta^{[b]}$ divides only when $e$ is a $\mathrm{Rollup}$ whose $b$ (Datadog's
\texttt{.as\_rate()}) holds, and a $\mathrm{Rollup}$ that no $\mathrm{Agg}$ encloses
divides its own values instead. Putting the divisor on the space aggregation rather
than on the rollup is not presentational: Remark~\ref{rem:as-rate-placement} measures
Datadog dividing the aggregated result, and the two placements differ for
$\mathit{op}=\mathsf{count}$. An earlier version of this definition divided per series,
which matched neither the vendor nor, after the differential test of
\S\ref{sec:differential}, our own emitter.
$\mathrm{Number}$, $\mathrm{Scale}$ and
$\mathrm{BinOp}$ are pointwise on matching label sets. A matcher is satisfied as
PromQL satisfies it, with $=\!\sim$ anchored over the whole value
(\S\ref{sec:disjunction}).
\end{definition}
