\begin{remark}[Two figures for the kernel, and the third that was wrong]\label{rem:kernel-figures}
A note on the two figures, because an earlier draft quoted a third. The $87\%$ it gave
is the cumulative column of Table~\ref{tab:implementation-order} --- the share of queries
a translator \emph{limited to the kernel's features} would complete, which is a
statement about a feature set. $\mathcal{K}$ is a statement about IR shape, and the two
differ: a query using a glob or a boolean scope is outside the first and inside the
second, since Lemma~\ref{lem:scope} covers every scope lowering admits. Computed from
the lowered IR rather than from the feature list, the kernel carries $8{,}363$
occurrences.
\end{remark}
