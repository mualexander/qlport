\apphead{Proofs, constructions, and the arguments behind them}{app:proofs}

Every proof in this paper is here but one, each named for the statement it discharges,
which is stated in the body at the point it is used. The exception is
Proposition~\ref{prop:decidable}, whose construction is the result it proves and which
is therefore built where \S\ref{sec:regular} states it. Collected with them is the apparatus those
arguments rest on: the IR's denotation, the lexical decisions behind the grammars, and
the remarks that record where an argument reached further than the result it was given
for, or did not reach as far. In the complete build all of it appears in place; here it
is collected, so the body reads as results and the arguments stand together where a
doubted result sends you.
